# NexaCore — Placement Preparation Platform

## Overview

**NexaCore** is a placement preparation website created as a practical project to simulate the **complete recruitment journey**, from initial resume screening to final onboarding.

The project was designed to provide a structured environment for practicing different stages of a typical placement process, including **resume screening, aptitude assessments, technical evaluation, interviews, HR rounds, selection, and onboarding**.

Rather than focusing on a single placement test, NexaCore models the recruitment process as a complete journey.

---

## Objective

The primary objective of NexaCore is to practice and understand the different stages involved in a modern recruitment and placement process.

The platform helps simulate:

```text id="x2n8pf"
Resume Submission
       ↓
Resume Screening
       ↓
Aptitude / Assessment
       ↓
Technical Evaluation
       ↓
Technical Interview
       ↓
HR Interview
       ↓
Selection
       ↓
Offer
       ↓
Onboarding
```

This approach provides practice across both **technical and non-technical aspects of placement preparation**.

---

## Placement Stages

### 1. Resume Screening

The journey begins with resume submission and screening.

This stage focuses on understanding how candidates may be evaluated based on factors such as:

* Education
* Skills
* Projects
* Experience
* Relevant technologies
* Overall profile

The stage helps users understand the importance of presenting relevant information clearly.

---

### 2. Aptitude Assessment

Candidates proceed to an assessment stage covering common placement-test areas such as:

* Quantitative aptitude
* Logical reasoning
* Analytical thinking
* Problem solving
* Verbal ability

The assessment stage is designed to simulate the type of screening tests commonly used during recruitment.

---

### 3. Technical Assessment

The technical stage focuses on evaluating programming and technical fundamentals.

Depending on the simulated role, this can include areas such as:

* Programming
* Data structures
* Algorithms
* Database concepts
* SQL
* Computer fundamentals
* Problem solving

---

### 4. Technical Interview

The technical interview stage focuses on evaluating how candidates explain and apply their knowledge.

Practice areas include:

* Programming concepts
* Projects
* SQL and databases
* Technical fundamentals
* Problem-solving approaches
* Questions based on the candidate's profile

---

### 5. HR Interview

The HR stage focuses on communication and behavioral aspects of recruitment.

Practice areas include:

* Self-introduction
* Strengths and weaknesses
* Career goals
* Project discussion
* Teamwork
* Conflict handling
* Situational questions
* Motivation and role expectations

---

### 6. Selection & Offer

After completing the evaluation stages, the candidate reaches the selection stage.

The simulated process represents the transition from interview evaluation to:

* Selection status
* Offer information
* Candidate confirmation
* Next steps

---

### 7. Onboarding

The final stage represents the transition from candidate to employee.

The onboarding stage can cover concepts such as:

* Joining information
* Documentation
* Employee details
* Role information
* Company onboarding process
* First-day preparation

NexaCore was created as a **placement-practice project** rather than simply a static portfolio website.

It combines **web development practice with a real-world recruitment workflow**, making the project both a technical exercise and a personal placement-preparation tool.
